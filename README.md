# ShopNest Store Business Analytics Dashboard 📊

## What is this project?
An interactive Power BI dashboard built on 9 interconnected 
e-commerce datasets, designed to answer key business questions 
across logistics, marketing, payments, ratings, and regional expansion.

---

## Problem it solves
ShopNest's sales, delivery, payment, rating, product, and regional 
data was spread across 9 separate datasets with no unified view. 
Decision-makers had no single place to answer business questions 
on performance, delays, revenue trends, or regional growth opportunities.
This dashboard consolidates everything into one interactive, 
drill-through-enabled report.

---

## Tools Used
| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Power BI | Dashboard creation and interactive visualisation |
| DAX | Custom measures and calculated columns |
| Power Query | Data transformation and relationship modelling |

---

## Business Questions Answered
1. Which product categories have the highest delayed orders?
2. What is the preferred payment method and its share?
3. How does revenue trend quarterly and yearly?
4. Which states drive the most revenue?
5. What is the average delivery time by region?
6. Which categories have the highest/lowest ratings?
7. How does Q2 compare to Q4 revenue?
8. Which regions show potential for expansion?

---

## Key Results
- ✅ Analysed ~R$16.01M in revenue across 2016–2018
- ✅ Credit cards account for 73.92% of all transactions
- ✅ `bed_bath_table` category leads with ~1,000 delayed orders
- ✅ Q2 revenue (~R$5M) exceeded Q4 (~R$2.8M) by 75%+
- ✅ Built bar, treemap, pie, line, and area visuals
- ✅ Drill-through and Year/Month slicers for dynamic filtering

---

## Dashboard Features
- 📌 9 datasets modelled with relationships
- 📌 Interactive slicers — Year and Month filters
- 📌 Drill-through for category and regional deep dives
- 📌 5 visual types: bar, treemap, pie, line, area charts
